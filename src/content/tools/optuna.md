Optuna is a powerful, easy-to-use library for hyperparameter tuning. It supports various samplers (TPE, CMA-ES) and pruning strategies, making it ideal for backtest parameter optimization. Its integration with PyTorch, TensorFlow, and scikit-learn is seamless.
