If you browse the MQL5 market, you'll notice that 90% of the top-selling Expert Advisors use either Grid or Martingale strategies. While they produce beautiful, smooth equity curves, they carry catastrophic hidden risks.

## The Grid Strategy

A grid system places buy and sell orders at regular intervals above and below a set price. It profits from the natural oscillation of the market. 
- **Pros:** Extremely profitable in ranging markets. Doesn't require precise entry timing.
- **Cons:** If a strong, unidirectional trend occurs without retracements, the floating drawdown can quickly exceed the account margin, resulting in a margin call.

## The Martingale Strategy

Martingale originated in 18th-century France as a betting strategy. In trading, it involves doubling your position size after every loss.
- **Pros:** You only need one winning trade to recover all previous losses plus a profit.
- **Cons:** A losing streak of 6-7 trades will exponentially increase lot sizes, requiring massive capital to sustain.

## The Verdict

Both systems are mathematically guaranteed to fail over an infinite timeline unless paired with a hard equity stop. If you choose to trade them, you must regularly withdraw your initial deposit.
