Trading Expert Advisors (EAs) from your home PC is a recipe for disaster. Power outages, Windows updates, and ISP latency can cause your trading bot to miss critical entries or fail to execute stop losses.

To trade professionally, you need a Forex VPS.

## What is a Forex VPS?

A Virtual Private Server (VPS) is a remote computer hosted in a data center that runs 24/7. You connect to it via Remote Desktop Protocol (RDP) and run your MetaTrader 4/5 instances there.

## Key Factors When Choosing a VPS

1. **Latency to Broker:** This is the most critical metric. Your VPS should be located in the same city (or even the same data center) as your broker's trading servers (e.g., Equinix NY4 or LD4). Aim for under 5ms latency.
2. **Uptime Guarantee:** Look for providers offering a 99.99% uptime SLA.
3. **RAM and CPU:** MT4 is single-threaded and consumes about 150MB of RAM per instance. If you run 4 terminals, you need at least 2GB of RAM.

## Recommended Specs

For a standard setup running 2-3 EAs, a VPS with 2 vCPUs, 2GB RAM, and an SSD drive is sufficient.

By running your [Algorithmic Trading Systems](/eas/) on a dedicated VPS, you ensure 24/5 execution without interruption.
